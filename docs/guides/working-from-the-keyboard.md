# Working from the keyboard

Daybird is built so you rarely need the mouse. This guide uses the Linux and Windows keys; on a Mac, use **⌘** where it says **Ctrl**. Single-letter keys work whenever you aren't typing in a box.

## Look at your week

1. Press **W** for Week, or **D**, **M**, **Y** or **A** for Day, Month, Year or Agenda.
2. Press **J** for the next week and **K** for the previous one (**Right** and **Left** do the same). **T** comes back to today.
3. Press **/** to search. Type a word from an event's title, place or notes, and press **Enter** to show the first match in its day. **Esc** brings the view back.

## Add an event

1. Press **N**. A new event appears at the next half hour, in the default calendar, with its title ready to type.
2. Type the title. **Tab** moves to the other fields: place, times, calendar, repeat, reminders and notes.
3. Press **Enter** in the title, or **Esc**, to close the popover. Everything saves as you type; **Esc** on an event you haven't touched takes it back.

## Change an event

1. Click an event to select it, or find it with **/** and **Enter**.
2. Press **Up** or **Down** to move it 15 minutes, **Shift+Up** or **Shift+Down** for an hour, and **Left** or **Right** for a day.
3. Press **Enter** to open it, **Ctrl+D** to duplicate it, **Ctrl+C** and **Ctrl+V** to copy and paste it, or **Delete** to send it to the Trash.
4. **Ctrl+Z** undoes any of these, and **Ctrl+Shift+Z** redoes.

## Everything else

Press **Ctrl+K** and type part of a command's name, such as "import" or "dark", then **Enter**. **Ctrl+/** shows every shortcut, and [Controls](../controls.md) lists them all.
